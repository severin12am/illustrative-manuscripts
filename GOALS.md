# Illustrative Manuscripts: final goals and handoff

Last work: 2026-09-24 (PRs #52-#56 merged). Owner: Mark (GitHub severin12am).

## The goal
An illustrated early-window manuscript census, year by year: every early manuscript or scrap with its scan, translation, find-spot, current location, scholarly links, and how it differs from today's text.
"100% done" means it's useful to three audiences: Bart Ehrman (research-grade), students of knowledge, and debaters/apologists.

## Where it stands (about 75-80% overall)
- Debaters/apologists ~85%, students ~80%, Ehrman research-grade ~60-65%.
- Greek NT 1-400 CE: CNTR transcriptions compared against the open SR GNT, about 37,592 word-level units, 108 witnesses (89 with CNTR text), 5,463 intentional-vs-error tags (provisional heuristic), and 10 curated famous passages.
- Hosted leaf images: Greek NT 91/108, Quran 15/19, Hebrew/LXX 19/24, Nag Hammadi 5/18 hosted (the rest are Claremont IIIF).
- Pages: About, Status, Use/Claims, Methods, Coverage, Cite, Teach (12 briefs), Sources, Famous, Variants, evidence maps for each corpus, global search, mobile nav.

## Rules to keep
1. Honesty over overclaim. This is an illustrative census, NOT a replacement for the INTF Liste, ECM/NA28, or Gurry's count. Never claim "we counted everything."
2. Reuse published open scholarship (CNTR, INTF/NTVMR, CSNTM, Gurry open materials, Tischendorf on IA, Corpus Coranicum, Leon Levy, Claremont). Don't reinvent collations.
3. Only legally reusable images: Commons public domain or clear CC, or open IIIF. No copyright-only museum photos.
4. Run cloud work on Cursor quota / Composer 2.5 only. Never use on-demand paid models.
5. NO outreach emails to Bart Ehrman or Quran experts until Mark explicitly says go. Don't draft or send them before then.
